Comment ça marche

Four steps, and we do three of them

1

Tell us what you're looking for

Your profession, your experience and where you want to work. If you've applied through us before, we already have your CV.

2

Pick how you pay

A flat 100 SAR a month while we search — or nothing at all until you accept an offer.

3

Pricing

Pick whichever suits you

Same service either way. The difference is only when you pay.

100 SAR a month

A flat monthly subscription for as long as you want us searching. Stop it whenever you like — there's no commitment and no commission at the end.

Pay only if it works

One month's salary, over 3 instalments

  • Split over three months, never more
  • If you're not hired, you owe nothing
