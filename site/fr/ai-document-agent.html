Document Advisor · inside your portal

Upload your documents. It finishes the rest.

Vendor forms, AML/KYC, bank forms, government questionnaires — filling them means copying the same company data for the hundredth time. The Document Advisor reads your CR, articles, IDs and statements once, then fills any form you throw at it — in place, with every value traceable to its source page.

What it does for you

For companies drowning in forms: banks, vendors, government portals and counterparties.

Reads everything at once

Documents with data, forms to fill, your stamp, even a screenshot of the requirements email — it classifies each file itself.

Extracts with provenance

Every value keeps its source, page, date and confidence. Conflicts become a question to you — never a silent guess.

Fills forms in place

Word, Excel and fillable PDFs come back filled — advisor-added data in blue, layout untouched, signature left for you.

Asks only for the gap

“I filled 87% — I still need the expected annual volume.” Legal declarations are never assumed.

One reusable company profile

CR + AOA + VAT + bank + IDs merge into one profile; your next request reuses it.

Private to your company

Files stored encrypted in your vault; downloads via short-lived signed links; no other account ever sees them.

Comment ça marche

1Upload

Drop in the data documents and the forms that need filling.

2Review

It reads, extracts, cross-checks and asks only for what is missing.

3Fill

Forms come back filled in place, with a QA pass before delivery.

4Package

Named per the checklist, zipped, signature left for you.

Where to use it

Document Advisor

  • Reads Arabic and English, fills in both directions
  • UBO and ownership math from your articles
  • Expiry check on every document
  • Natural-language edits: “Section 9 all No”
  • QA pass before anything reaches you

Common questions

Why can't I chat with it here?

Because your documents and conversations belong inside your account, scoped to your company — not on a public page. Open it from your client portal.

Does it sign for me?

Never. It never fabricates a signature and never assumes legal declarations — it asks, records your explicit confirmation, and leaves the signature to you.
