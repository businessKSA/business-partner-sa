Quick knowledge guides

Knowledge guide

Foreign company or Gulf company?

A Gulf national is treated almost like a Saudi (no customs duties between GCC states), while a foreign company needs a MISA license and attested documents. We identify the right track for your nationality and activity.

Read more Knowledge guide

Industrial license: preliminary or final?

The preliminary license is for planning and setup stages; the final is for factories ready to operate and produce. Both may grant customs exemptions on equipment and raw materials.

Read more Knowledge guide

Premium Residency: which product fits you?

Permanent with a one-time fee or renewable annual, plus category products (talent, skill, investor, entrepreneur, property owner). The smart agent recommends the best fit for your financial situation.

Read more Knowledge guide

Why a feasibility study first?

It's a core requirement for an industrial license and Industrial Fund financing, and helps you secure funding from banks and investors through financial, marketing and technical analysis.

Read more Knowledge guide

Remote trademark registration

Individuals and companies inside and outside the Kingdom can register their marks with SAIP without attending in person, with 10-year renewable protection.

Read more Knowledge guide

Contractor classification & the government projects gateway

A classification certificate is a core requirement for participating in major government projects, reflecting your financial, technical and administrative capabilities.
