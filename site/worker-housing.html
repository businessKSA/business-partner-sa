The regulations

Why random housing became a real risk

From MoMaH's supervision guide for collective housing (ministerial decision 4600218548/1 — 1446H / 2025):

20+ workers

counts as collective housing and requires an operating license

4 ministries

permanent committees inspect worker housing (MoMaH, Interior, Health, HR)

Every quarter

a scheduled inspection round for every housing license

Ewaa platform

violations are logged electronically; fines double on repeat

Violation Fine (Category-1 cities) Class
Operating collective housing without an operating licenseup to 10,000 SARMajor — facility may be closed
No clinic/nurse for capacity above 1,000 workersup to 8,000 SARMajor
Violating the minimum area per worker in bedroomsup to 5,000 SARMajor
Bathrooms below minimum (toilet + sink + shower per 8 workers)up to 5,000 SARMajor
Exceeding licensed capacityup to 5,000 SARMajor
No air conditioning and heating in bedroomsup to 4,000 SARMajor
No resident register and medical insurance per residentup to 4,000 SARMajor
Not separating women's housing from men'sup to 3,000 SARMajor
No equipped isolation room for sick residentsup to 3,000 SARMajor
No bed and wardrobe per workerup to 2,000 SARMajor

Source: MoMaH supervision guide (momah.gov.sa). Fines scale by municipality category and double on repeat.
