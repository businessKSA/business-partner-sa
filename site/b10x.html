B10X Relocation

We don't just relocate your business. We relocate the people behind it.

🛫 Pre-arrival

Needs assessment, city & area selection, housing and school requirements, commute and arrival planning.

🛬 Arrival

Airport reception, executive car, temporary residence, SIM, orientation and Account Manager introduction.

🏠 Executive housing

Apartment, villa, compound or serviced residence — matched to budget, office, family and lifestyle.

🏘️ Workforce housing

Licensed group housing with maintenance, utilities, transport and catering coordination.

🏫 Family & schools

International school search (British, American, IB) by budget, age and location — shortlist, visits, applications. Admission is not guaranteed.

🚗 Transport, banking & settling-in

Chauffeur/leasing coordination, bank appointment support, city orientation — through departure and exit support.

ℹ️

Flights, hotels, rent, school fees and all third-party costs are separate unless priced explicitly.
