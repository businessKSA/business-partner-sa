服务说明

There are seven Premium Residency routes, each with its own conditions, fees and documents — and picking the wrong one means a refusal months later. A session that reads your case, names your route, and leaves you with a written report of conditions, documents, fees and an execution plan.

所需文件

  • A brief on your nationality and activity
  • Any licences, assets or qualifications you hold
  • Any earlier application or decision concerning you

Business Partner 的服务特点

  • Reading your case: nationality, activity, assets and qualifications
  • Naming the right Premium Residency route for you
  • Setting out the route's conditions, documents and fees as published
  • A written execution plan with steps, timelines and responsibilities
  • An estimate of fees and charges before you start
