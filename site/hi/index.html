Every line below is an obligation with a date on it. Missing the date is what costs money — not the fee itself.

Iqamas & work permits

Renewals tracked per employee and filed before they lapse, with the fees paid from your balance.

GOSI & platform fees

Monthly subscriptions and the government platforms your company is registered on, settled on schedule.

Municipality & commercial licences

Payroll

Salaries disbursed from your balance on your cycle, so wage-protection status stays clean.

Violations

Reviewed, eligibility for objection studied, prepared, filed and followed up. No promise of cancellation — the decision is the authority's.
