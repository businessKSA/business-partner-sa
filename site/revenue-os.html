FAQ

Before you start

Does pipeline value mean guaranteed revenue?

No. It is a target value for qualified opportunities and depends on offer quality, the market, response speed and closing factors.

When is the success fee due?

The agreement defines the trigger. The preferred model ties the fee to revenue actually collected.

What is the difference between the success fee and the closing add-on?

The success fee relates to generating and managing the opportunity; the closing add-on relates to actually taking part in negotiation and signing.

Can we run the supplier track only?

Yes. Customers, suppliers, distributors or partners can each run as an independent track.
