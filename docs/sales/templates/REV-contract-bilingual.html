4. Monthly Deliverables
The First Party commits to a monthly minimum by package: REV-LAUNCH: 4 qualified leads, 4 vendor registrations and 4 meetings; REV-GROWTH: 8, 8 and 8; REV-PRO: 16, 16 and 16. “Qualified lead”: an entity with valid contact details (email and/or mobile) and a stated need or clear rationale; it is deemed accepted unless the Client rejects it in writing, with reasons, within 5 business days of handover. “Vendor registration”: a complete submission of the registration application, with documents, on the target company’s platform, not the company’s acceptance; it requires the Client to provide complete documents on time, failing which any shortfall is not the First Party’s default. “Meeting”: a meeting actually held, or arranged and missed by the Client or the entity; such a miss is not the First Party’s default. The minimum is a monthly commitment and the sole remedy for missing it is completion in the following month, failing which a credit is applied to the next invoice; on expiry or termination the credit is settled in cash or deducted from the last invoice, and it does not count toward the liability cap. Monthly performance report and a list of companies, portals and registration emails by client activity.		4. المخرجات الشهرية
يلتزم الطرف الأول شهرياً بحد أدنى بحسب الباقة: REV-LAUNCH: 4 فرص مؤهلة و4 تسجيلات مورد و4 اجتماعات؛ REV-GROWTH: 8 و8 و8؛ REV-PRO: 16 و16 و16. «فرصة مؤهلة»: جهة ببيانات تواصل صالحة (إيميل و/أو جوال) وحاجة معلنة أو مبرر واضح، وتُعدّ مقبولة ما لم يرفضها العميل كتابةً مع بيان السبب خلال 5 أيام عمل من تسليمها. «تسجيل مورد»: تقديم مكتمل لطلب التسجيل على منصة الشركة المستهدفة بمستنداته، لا قبول الشركة له، ويشترط توفير العميل مستنداته كاملة في الوقت المناسب وإلا لا يُعدّ النقص تقصيراً من الطرف الأول. «اجتماع»: اجتماع عُقد فعلاً أو جرى ترتيبه فتخلّف عنه العميل أو الجهة، ولا يُعدّ تخلّفهما تقصيراً من الطرف الأول. الحد الأدنى التزام شهري، وجزاء الإخلال به حصراً: استكمال المخرجات الناقصة في الشهر التالي، فإن لم تُستكمل يُحتسب رصيد دائن في الفاتورة التالية، وعند انتهاء العقد يُسوّى الرصيد نقداً أو يُخصم من آخر فاتورة، ولا يدخل هذا الرصيد في حساب سقف المسؤولية. تقرير أداء شهري وقائمة الشركات وصفحات وإيميلات التسجيل بحسب نشاط العميل.‏
5. Success Fee
The First Party earns a success fee when the Client concludes a deal arising from a lead it delivered, by deal value: up to SAR 1,000,000: 10%; 1,000,000 to 5,000,000: 5%; above 5,000,000: 2.5%. If it participates in closing, an additional 2.5% applies on the same deal. The fee is before tax and 15% VAT is added. Value basis: signed contract or purchase order value, excl. VAT. The tier applies to the whole value, and the parties acknowledge the effect of this at the tier boundaries. Due within 14 days. “Deal arising from a lead”: a deal concluded with the entity whose lead was delivered in writing (email or portal), or with its affiliates or related companies, within 12 months of handover, including purchase orders actually issued during that period under it, whether renewals, repeat orders or call-offs under a framework agreement, but not estimated values. “Participation in closing”: a written request from the Client for the First Party’s participation, or its actual attendance at a negotiation meeting at the Client’s request. The Client notifies the First Party in writing within 7 days of concluding the deal and provides a copy of the contract or purchase order (or its value only, if the price is confidential); if it does not, the First Party may rely on its own evidence (CC copies, supplier portal, public award) and may request an annual written statement of deal values certified by the Client’s CFO. The Client shall not agree with the entity to delay closing or restructure the deal to avoid the fee. The fee remains due to the First Party even after the contract expires or is terminated or cancelled for any reason, on every lead it delivered while the contract was in force, whenever the deal is concluded with the same entity or its affiliates within the attribution period, whether the Client closes it alone or with the First Party’s participation; this clause survives expiry of the contract, and termination does not extinguish an accrued right.		5. نسبة النجاح
